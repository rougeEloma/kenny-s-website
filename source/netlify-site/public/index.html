Our story

Sharing the luxury of your life

Since 1993, Kenny's Group has brought the world's leading watch, jewellery and fashion houses to Central Africa. From our flagship at R.R. House on Boulevard du 30 Juin in Kinshasa to K-Galerie in Pointe-Noire, we present tailoring, timepieces and accessories to clients who appreciate craftsmanship.
