Since 1993

How it all began

Kenny embarked on his journey in 1993, armed with steadfast determination and a modest store.

Kenny's Group is a well-established lifestyle retail company that has operated since 1993. Its portfolio of more than 50 international franchise concepts spans several African countries, with stores offering leading fashion and accessory brands alongside beauty and cosmetic products.

The group has become a one-stop destination for fashion enthusiasts, with a loyal customer base. Its commitment to quality products and service has made it a significant regional employer.

The founder

Mr Kenny Rawtani

Founder and Chairman of Kenny's Group

With more than 30 years of experience in African markets, Kenny Rawtani began his career in Congo-Brazzaville in 1993 before settling in Kinshasa, where he founded Kenny's International.

The company started with high-end menswear and ready-to-wear, led by its own bespoke tailoring brand, Kenny's. It has since grown into a leading franchise developer across Africa, with a portfolio of top international brands.
